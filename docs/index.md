# Reindeer Grand Prix 🦌🛷

Drive a sleigh round a snowy track. Record your driving. Teach a small neural network to drive like you, then race it on a live leaderboard against the rest of Cambridge.

**[Play now →](https://reindeer-gp.dcaisoc.workers.dev)** No installation needed: everything up to your first leaderboard entry happens in the browser.

## Three ways in

| Level | You are… | You'll use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Elf** | New to machine learning. | The browser only: drive, click **Train**, submit. No code. |
| **Reindeer** | Able to write some Python. | [Notebook 01](notebooks.md) (PyTorch, on Colab) and the [SDK](sdk.md). |
| **Santa** | Keen or experienced. | Anything you like, as long as it exports to the [bot format](formats/bot.md). |

Your level puts you on the matching leaderboard; you can move up, but not down once you've submitted.
