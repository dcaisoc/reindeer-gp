# Code of conduct

Reindeer Grand Prix is for everyone, especially people new to machine learning. We expect all participants, contributors and organisers to:

- be kind and patient, particularly with beginners and their questions;
- give feedback on work, not on people;
- respect that people come from different backgrounds, colleges, subjects and experience levels;
- not harass, demean or exclude anyone, in person or online.

Display names and team names must be suitable for a public leaderboard. Referees may rename or hide names that are not.

If something goes wrong, contact the organisers at dcaisoc@outlook.com. Reports are handled in confidence. Organisers may warn, remove content, or remove people from the competition.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
